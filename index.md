A curated group of 6 to 10 builders, writers, and engineers gather at an extended-stay suite property (like Hilton Home2 Suites). Everyone gets their own private room, kitchen, and dedicated workspace. That removes social pressure and logistical drag, while the communal energy stays where it belongs: at the edges of the day.
